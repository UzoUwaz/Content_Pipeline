- Thick, uneven black outlines
- Stick figure humans with round heads and line bodies

- Mostly white empty space
- Red arrows or red question marks when they help
- No realistic shading, no 3D, no cinematic lighting
- No Disney style, no anime style, no realistic cartoon style
- No polished illustration, no professional vector art
- No detailed backgrounds, no complex textures, no realistic humans
- No glossy or modern design

Format: horizontal 16:9 wide frame. Clean, readable, centered. Do not crop important objects. Leave space around characters and objects. Avoid glitches, broken anatomy, unreadable text, and messy overlapping objects.
